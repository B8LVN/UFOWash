name: Small garage progression
description: Owner's starting-scale and visible-growth requirements for UFO WASH.
---

Start on a small planet washing UFOs with a low-cost setup inside the player's
garage. Grow from there, hire someone, and upgrade the garage's visuals when
upgrading the garage. The owner wants major UI/UX, 3D-model and surrounding-world improvements.

**Why:** The owner explicitly specified this direction.

**How to apply:** Make the initial garage genuinely modest, with readable physical
growth tied to purchases. Do not start with the already-developed service complex
that the player should work toward. Treat detailed progression tiers and art
proposals as proposals until accepted.
